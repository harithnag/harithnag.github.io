Research.

My research interests lie in mathematics, particularly in the connections between algebraic structures, topology, and geometry. This page introduces the areas I work on and the questions that guide my research.

Research interests

01 / ALGEBRA

Representation Theory

Studying how algebraic structures can be understood through their actions on vector spaces and the representations they admit.

02 / TOPOLOGY

Algebraic Topology

Exploring algebraic invariants that capture topological information and help distinguish mathematical spaces.

03 / GEOMETRY

Characteristic Classes

Investigating characteristic classes as tools for studying vector bundles and the geometric information they encode.

04 / CONNECTIONS

Groups, Bundles & Geometry

Interested in the relationships between group representations, cohomology, vector bundles, and geometric constructions.

Current work

My current work explores questions at the intersection of representation theory and characteristic classes, with an emphasis on constructions arising from finite groups and their cohomology.

Mathematics is a conversation between structures, ideas, and the connections that emerge between them.
