Research.

I am a mathematics PhD student interested in algebraic topology and representation theory. My work explores connections between algebraic structures, cohomology, and characteristic classes. This page introduces the mathematical areas that shape my research.

Research interests

01 · ALGEBRA

Representation Theory

The study of algebraic structures through their actions on vector spaces, with particular interest in representations of finite groups.

02 · TOPOLOGY

Algebraic Topology

The use of algebraic tools to study topological spaces, spaces of geometric interest, and the invariants that reveal their underlying structure.

03 · CHARACTERISTIC CLASSES

Characteristic Classes

Cohomological invariants associated with vector bundles, and their role in understanding geometric and algebraic constructions.

04 · CONNECTIONS

Finite Groups and Cohomology

Exploring how representations of finite groups interact with group cohomology, characteristic classes, and transfer-like constructions.

Current research

My current work concerns characteristic classes associated with representations and constructions involving finite groups. I am interested in understanding how these classes behave under different algebraic and cohomological operations.

Exploring the connections between algebraic structures and the geometric information they carry.
