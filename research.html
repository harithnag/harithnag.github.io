Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of representation theory, topology, and characteristic classes. I am interested in understanding how algebraic structures interact with topological invariants, particularly in the setting of finite groups.

Research Interests

My current interests focus on the relationships between representations of finite groups, cohomology, and characteristic classes. These subjects provide different ways to study the structure of groups and the geometric information encoded by their representations.

  • Representation theory of finite groups
  • Algebraic and geometric topology
  • Characteristic classes
  • Group cohomology

Current Research

My work explores characteristic classes associated with representations and constructions involving finite groups. I am particularly interested in how cohomological operations and representation-theoretic constructions influence the resulting characteristic classes.

Research Background

My interests bring together algebraic methods and topological ideas. I aim to understand how representations give rise to characteristic classes and how these classes behave under natural constructions and maps between groups.
