Research

My research interests lie in algebraic topology and representation theory, particularly in the study of finite groups and characteristic classes.

Research interests

I am interested in the connections between algebraic structures and topological invariants, especially how representations of finite groups give rise to characteristic classes.

  • Representation theory of finite groups
  • Algebraic topology
  • Characteristic classes
  • Group cohomology

Current work

My work explores characteristic classes associated with representations of finite groups and the behavior of these classes under constructions involving groups and their representations.
